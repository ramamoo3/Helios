# Circuit designs to build on

Project Helios Rev A · comparison for the battery cabinets, DC power stages and three-phase base

## What to use first

**Use Libre Solar BMS C1 as the starting point for the battery cabinet and Infineon's 3.3 kW converter as the comparison for the base-unit battery port.** The former has editable KiCad files, a hardware license and test results for a pack in our voltage and current class. The latter publishes schematics, parts and measurements for moving kilowatts in both directions at 40–60 V. They cover different sides of the same Helios cable. The Helios schematics in this workspace were drawn independently. [BMS C1 schematic](https://github.com/LibreSolar/bms-c1/blob/main/build/bms-c1.pdf) · [Infineon converter application note](https://www.infineon.com/assets/row/public/documents/24/42/infineon-evaluation-board-eval-3k3w-bidi-psfb-applicationnotes-en.pdf)

## What the comparison changes in Rev A

1. **Give the cabinet and converter separate current limits.** At 40 V the Helios simulation drew 68.2 A average from the pack and reached 110.7 A peak in the primary transfer inductor. Compare pack average with the BMS's continuous allowance and ripple specification. Size converter sensing, switches and magnetics from the switching waveform. A 60 A discharge allowance would cap battery input at 2.4 kW before conversion losses. The controller must reduce the requested power in that case. [Helios simulation results](battery-port/README.md) · [C1 current specifications](https://github.com/LibreSolar/bms-c1)
2. **Keep the common-link voltage open through inverter selection.** The 400 V references offer tested battery stages. The 700 V Vienna design offers a tested three-phase utility front end. Resolve the inverter's phase-to-neutral topology and modulation headroom, the PV operating window and charger design before committing to a link voltage or transformer. [Infineon converter](https://www.infineon.com/evaluation-board/EVAL-3K3W-BIDI-PSFB) · [TI Vienna rectifier](https://www.ti.com/lit/ug/tiducj0e/tiducj0e.pdf)
3. **Adopt the published test methods.** Start with a cell simulator and check cell limits, separate charge/discharge inhibits and recovery. For the converter, record winding peaks, switch-node overshoot, response to lost gate bias and temperature under sustained current. Then exercise two independent ports on the same link while one pack lowers its allowable charge current. [BMS test procedure](https://github.com/LibreSolar/bms-c1/blob/main/testing/v0.3/README.md) · [Infineon application note](https://www.infineon.com/assets/row/public/documents/24/42/infineon-evaluation-board-eval-3k3w-bidi-psfb-applicationnotes-en.pdf)
