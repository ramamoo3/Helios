# Project Helios software verification

Fabrication is on hold while the complete design is developed and verified in software. Before release, each electrical block needs an executable model, measured or published inputs, numeric pass criteria, fault cases and a review of what the model omits. Tie results to the revision of the schematic, board, BOM and firmware. Record changes that invalidate a previous result.

| --- | --- | --- |

CI is configured to run the base circuit regression on ngspice 42 and the two compiled firmware images against the Unicorn register fixture. The KiCad 10 job checks ERC, DRC and schematic parity on the parent and rear-bypass projects, then regenerates and refills the return-plane trial and runs a DC extraction smoke test. The locally published vendor replays use ngspice 47 with TI models in PSpice compatibility mode or Xyce with the documented adaptations. The firmware fixture uses scripted peripheral behavior. Actual HRTIM timing, independent hardware shutdown and physical fault response need bench correlation. Record the solver version, model source and result for each claim. [CI workflow](.github/workflows/verify.yml) · [Circuit checks](circuits/battery-port/README.md) · [Firmware tests](circuits/battery-port/BENCH-HRTIM.md)

The software release evidence supports fabrication review. It cannot establish physical shock safety, insulation integrity or workmanship. After fabrication, a qualified power-electronics lab should own the high-energy power-up and safety tests. [Safe isolation and stored electrical energy](https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/priced/hsg85.pdf)

## Repository audit follow-up

The local repeat rechecked the original findings, the populated BOM, the source fingerprints and the unchanged geometry. This revision has local check results. The GitHub Actions results apply to the previously pushed revision.

| Finding | Verified change or remaining work |
| --- | --- |
| Return planes, power copper, clearance and assembly | The parent has explicit routing and interdomain clearance rules. The generated candidate has separate filled return planes, wider inner winding paths, four mounting holes and three fiducials. Native KiCad reported zero ERC violations, DRC violations, opens and parity mismatches. The 52 DC result groups and the 256-via land check reproduced. The 1.5 and 1 mm meshes returned 7.61 and 7.60 nH for secondary A at 10 MHz. The independent series solve agreed, and the filament geometry warnings remain. Steady-state thermal limits and actual assembly fit remain open. [PCB trial and extraction](circuits/battery-port/BENCH-PCB.md#return-plane-trial-and-design-rules) |
| Power switch selection | Both TO-220 devices passed the standalone startup matrix. The routed 100 V TO-220 candidate still has inconclusive high-side-short and forced-current cases. The SON rear-bypass coupon passed 16 reduced-driver resistive and short runs with a 39 Ω low-side gate resistor. An inductive candidate passed both directions at 25 °C and 100 °C at 125, 250 and 500 ps in the reduced driver. The highest drain peak was 33.44 V. An ST diode model also passed a hot high-side check with assumed loop inductance. Layout extraction, faults, safe operating area and independent-model verification remain open. The vendor-driver rail still dipped below 9.2 V at finer steps with the direct pin bypass. [TO-220 results](circuits/battery-port/BENCH-PARTS.md#100-v-to-220-candidate) · [SON inductive trial](circuits/battery-port/BENCH-SECONDARY-PROTECTED.md#inductive-commutation-stress) |
| BOM and shunts | All 29 audited capacitor positions carry matching manufacturer, MPN and voltage fields in the KiCad schematic and PCB. The native BOM export reproduced the expected counts. The 0.5 mΩ parent R30 and the 5 mΩ isolated-coupon shunt have distinct roles. The measured effective bootstrap capacitance and independent verification of the populated parts remain open. [Bench parts](circuits/battery-port/BENCH-PARTS.md) · [Shunt model](circuits/battery-port/BENCH-SHUNT-TRANSFER.md) |
| Firmware, CI and reproducibility | The workflow pins ngspice, Zig and CMSIS and exercises the circuits, emulator, stable KiCad and link checks. The previous audit branch passed all hosted jobs. The current local run built and emulated both images and rejected a deliberately wrong HRTIM compare-event bit. Actual timing and fault response need a capture on the target. [Firmware procedure](circuits/battery-port/BENCH-HRTIM.md) · [Workflow runs](https://github.com/ramamoo3/Helios/actions/workflows/verify.yml) |
| Results, variants and lint | The older replay and sensitivity reports retain their original fingerprints. The new repeat records results from the current scripts. Five layout extractors use a common coupled-copper solver path and reproduced their original matrices and loop values. The archived coupon index links both DFN schematics. Pyflakes passed on the project scripts. [Coupon index](circuits/battery-port/README.md#secondary-leg-experiments) · [Repeat report](circuits/battery-port/bench-secondary-isolated-routed-audit.json) |
| Independent DC interruption | The linked 12 V and 24 V model exercised both cutoffs, independent PWM inhibition and the separate precharge guard in charge and discharge. All ten cases reproduced their expected outcomes at two steps, including controls that bypassed PWM inhibition, bypassed one cutoff or shorted both precharge switches. The actual cutoff devices, isolation, fuse coordination and precharge recovery still need validation. [Linked bench cutoff](circuits/battery-port/BENCH-LOCAL-SHUTDOWN.md#linked-cutoff-and-pwm-inhibit) |
| Pilot, charger, standby and enclosure | The middle and heavy pilot traces exceed the four-pack overnight window. The charger has a 10 kW AC input budget and reduces battery charging as house demand rises. The current product ratings are consolidated. The standby deck measures 3.78 W modeled net loss during zero-shift switching, which disappears when both bridges stop. The complete energized standby draw and enclosure depth still require qualification. [Architecture](HELIOS-ARCHITECTURE.md) |
| Independent review | Engineering sign-off remains open. Record the independent reviewer, reviewed revision, findings and resolution before fabrication. |
