# Project Helios completion checklist

The deliverable is one wall-mounted base containing the inverter, solar inputs, import-only utility charger and four independent battery ports, plus one to four matching 5.12 kWh LFP cabinets. The Madurai pilot uses three phases plus neutral. The product family also calls for a single-phase starter. Completion of the pilot and readiness to manufacture have separate acceptance tests.

### Paths traced and findings to close

1. **Inductor assembly needs a decision before release.** Three existing 0.40 mm through drills intersect the L1 top-side solder lands. Pad 1 has drills at (65.300, 93.500) and (66.650, 93.500) mm on PRI_A. Pad 2 has one at (74.100, 93.775) mm on CT_OUTBOUND. The earlier drill check considered newly added signal vias. The full-board check includes the original power routing. Open holes can draw solder from these joints. Relocate the vias while preserving the current path, or obtain the assembler's approved filled/capped via process and include it in the fabrication drawing and cost. Repeat the drill-to-land and power-path checks on the chosen revision.
2. **Bridge, windings and driver pinout.** J3.1 reaches R30.1 and R30.4 feeds the primary bridge. PRI_A connects Q1 source, Q2 drain, U2 HS and L1.1. L1.2 reaches T2.4, and T2.3 reaches the dotted primary terminal T1.1. The opposite primary node reaches T1.2. T1.3 and T1.4 serve the secondary legs. G1–G8 each reach MOSFET pin 1 through the intended series resistor and have a pull-down. U2's enable, HS, outputs and VSS agree with the TI DRC-package pinout. Qualify the custom winding dot convention, TO-220 live drain tabs, shared source impedance and local bypass at the final placement. [Bench parts](circuits/battery-port/BENCH-PARTS.md) · [TI driver pinout, figure 5-1](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ucc27282.pdf)
3. **Current trip and permission.** T2.1 feeds R11/R12 and the filtered signal reaches both comparator inputs. The latch drives PERMIT_P to U2.6, U102.6 and J5.10. U7 carries permission across isolation and U13 separately qualifies SEC_EN for U202.6 and U302.6. PERMIT_P contains 140.48 mm of total branch copper and three vias. SEC_EN contains 134.69 mm and four vias. These totals characterize copper for extraction. Determine worst-case timing from a current crossing through actual cessation of device current. The winding CT cannot detect a shoot-through loop confined to one half-bridge.
4. **Shunt, telemetry and controller.** R30.2 and R30.3 reach U1.2 and U1.3 respectively and also branch to J1. KP and KN contain 68.85 and 75.50 mm of total copper, with two vias each. The shunt, INA240 PW input polarity and ADC output routing agree. Check common-mode pickup, return impedance, filtering and ADC sample settling against a defined error budget. J5.7/8 carry telemetry for later closed-loop control. J5.11 requests bootstrap precharge and J5.12/13 return independent acknowledgements. J5 is an unkeyed 1×13 header. Document pin 1, cable orientation, logic levels and ground reference in the bench fixture. [INA240 PW pin table](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ina240.pdf) · [Controller mappings](circuits/battery-port/BENCH-HRTIM.md)
5. **Switching margin is unresolved.** The existing seven vendor-model baseline cases and four inconclusive added-inductance cases are historical results. This audit reran the standard ngspice suite and firmware emulation. The four previously inconclusive cases need repeatable convergence with extracted impedances. Use peak local rail current, Vgs, Vds, negative HS excursion and shutdown energy to judge the design. Hold the gate resistance and protection decisions open until those results exist. [Vendor-model results and limitations](circuits/battery-port/BENCH-BOOTSTRAP.md)

The comparison counted unique numbered electrical pins by reference and pin number. Route lengths above are sums of segment centerlines on a net, including branches, excluding via barrels and package internals. For a repeat audit, export the root schematic with `kicad-cli sch export netlist --format kicadxml`, compare its node tuples with the footprint pads, run `kicad-cli sch erc` and `kicad-cli pcb drc --schematic-parity` with `--exit-code-violations`, then run the [circuit regression](circuits/battery-port/verify.py) and [firmware build and emulation](circuits/battery-port/BENCH-HRTIM.md). Record the tool versions and source revision.

## 1. Establish the design inputs

- [ ] **Pilot measurement set.** Collect bills with import/export registers and billing periods, solar generation data for the same intervals, per-phase load and overnight energy, and starting-current traces for ACs, pump and lift. Confirm the lift's emergency rescue and possible braking-energy behavior with its manufacturer. Record actual panel labels, existing inverter and string wiring, service capacity, earthing, installation temperature, shade and mounting location. Calculate what two additional panels would actually add.
- [ ] **Configuration and rating matrix.** Map 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20 kWh/day examples to panels, cabinet count, usable energy and backup duration. Specify per-phase and total kW/kVA, surge duration, idle draw, allowed transfer interruption, charger input limit, operating environment, service life and cost. Define automatic behavior when the installed packs can supply less than the household demands.
- [ ] **System electrical design.** Draw a single-line diagram for both service variants and the existing-PV retrofit. Assign neutral formation, isolation, earthing, protective devices and switching for every source mode. Select inverter topology and common DC-link voltage together. Define which converter regulates the link in each mode. Calculate normal and fault currents, energy absorption, protection coordination, isolation distances and thermal budgets. The 700 V link and 125 A simulated trip threshold are study values.
- [ ] **Conformity and interface plan.** With the installer and test lab, identify the electrical installation, battery, inverter/charger, EMC, enclosure, transport and rooftop-service requirements applicable to the intended product. Resolve the existing net-metered installation and any required approvals before site work. Write pack power/communications, PV, grid, AC output, cabling and firmware interface specifications. ## 2. Build the battery subsystem first

- [x] **Bench protection schematic and routed shutdown chain.** The current transformer, window comparators, latch, isolated permission, drivers and bias monitoring are connected on the bench PCB.
- [ ] **Protection timing qualification.** Set worst-case thresholds and a maximum fault-clearing time from sensor errors, current rise, magnetics saturation and switch ratings. Demonstrate it on the extracted bench model and separately on the production design.
- [ ] **Low-voltage prototypes and fixtures.** Build one current-limited 12 V ↔ 24 V converter, then a second independently controlled port. Provide a finite DC link, programmable sources/loads, fault injection, isolated measurements and capture of individual gate signals. Verify both power directions, startup, peak current, shutdown delay, restart, unequal sharing and disabling one port. Implement closed-loop current and link-voltage control and verify load redistribution and overload response.
- [ ] **Production-intent battery cabinet.** Select the actual 16S, 100 Ah cells or qualified pack, BMS and charge/discharge limits. Build the cell restraints, interconnects, insulation, thermal sensors, balancing, current sensing, fuse, service disconnect, switching and keyed power/comms harness. Define identity, live limits, state-of-charge reporting, missing-message handling and firmware compatibility. Characterize usable capacity and thermal behavior inside the enclosure.
- [ ] **Full-power battery port and four-port assembly.** Select switches, transformer, transfer inductance, drivers, shunt and current sensor. Complete per-port isolation, precharge, voltage verification, fault disconnect, sensing-supply isolation, busbars and connectors. Qualify one port throughout the released pack-voltage and temperature range in charge and discharge. Scale to four ports with individual inhibits, current limits and commissioning. Verify that adding a cabinet increases capacity and that removing an available port reduces the allowed power.

## 3. Build the complete base

- [ ] **DC link and auxiliary power.** Design link capacitors for ripple and transients, voltage sensing, precharge, controlled discharge and overvoltage response. Define how full batteries and sudden load removal are handled. Build housekeeping supplies and startup logic so the unit can boot from each supported source and recover after protective pack shutdown. Measure standby draw with empty and occupied ports.
- [ ] **Solar MPPT stage.** Build up to two independent trackers targeting 3 kWp each. Include PV disconnection and protection, voltage/current sensing, short-string startup, regulation over the roof temperature range, and curtailment when loads disappear or batteries are full. Validate the minimum two-panel configuration, the pilot's proposed 5 kWp array and the product maximum. Document how the existing rooftop inverter and panels are treated during retrofit.
- [ ] **Import-only utility charger.** Build the service-matched AC input stage, rectification, power-factor correction, filtering, protection and per-phase import limiting. The upper design targets adjustable input up to 10 kW AC and up to 5 kW DC battery charging, subject to concurrent house load, site and BMS limits. Verify operation at low line, phase loss and recovery, and the physical reverse-power barrier.
- [ ] **House inverter and AC distribution.** Build a grid-forming 4 kVA phase module, output filter and metering. For the pilot, synchronize three phases at 50 Hz, establish a load-carrying neutral, and support uneven loads within 4 kVA per phase and 8 kVA total. Validate voltage quality, nonlinear loads, surge, overload, output short circuits and downstream fault clearing. Qualify the single-phase installation separately.
- [ ] **Transfer and maintenance bypass.** Build break-before-make switching, mechanical/electrical interlocks, source qualification, position feedback and service isolation. Specify neutral switching and protective-earth continuity for the site. Verify wrong phase sequence, stuck contacts, missing neutral and grid loss during bypass. Qualify residual-current and overcurrent protection in inverter and bypass modes.
- [ ] **Controller, metering and local interface.** Build the supervisory board, real-time control interfaces, watchdogs, isolated communications, per-phase meters and event storage. Implement startup, shutdown, re-arm and recovery states. Implement solar priority, per-pack dispatch, reserve, forecast-based grid top-up, fallback rules and load shedding. Provide local status, source flows, reserve setting, available power, alarms, installer commissioning and firmware update/recovery. Verify basic operation offline.
- [ ] **Enclosures and cooling.** Design the matching base and battery wall boxes around service access, mounting loads, cell restraints, cable bend radius, protective barriers and earthing. Size heatsinks and ventilation, define thermal derating, and test representative ambient temperature, loss of cooling and noise. Create the harness drawings, labels and instructions for de-energized expansion.
- [ ] **PCB and assembly release for prototypes.** For each board, finish schematics, footprints, BOM and sourcing, PCB stack-up, high-current loops, isolation layout, test points and drawings. Run native electrical and layout checks and review the magnetics, current paths, protection and mechanical fit. Generate fabrication and assembly files, procure parts, assemble, inspect and bring up boards individually. Iterate on measured faults before integration.

## 4. Prove the complete system and commission the pilot
