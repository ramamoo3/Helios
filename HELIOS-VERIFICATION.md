# Project Helios software verification

Fabrication is on hold while the complete design is developed and verified in software. Before release, each electrical block needs an executable model, measured or published inputs, numeric pass criteria, fault cases and a review of what the model omits. Tie results to the revision of the schematic, board, BOM and firmware. Record changes that invalidate a previous result.

| Design area | Evidence now | Evidence still needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12 V ↔ 24 V bench battery port | Schematic checks, 12 switching startup cases, eight integrated current-trip and bias-loss cases, and full-converter TI model coverage for the four bootstrap supplies. The wired decoder passed all 128 input combinations. Both firmware images require independent acknowledgements before PWM. Seven baseline vendor-model runs measured less than 0.2 µA of simultaneous forward channel current, including the 3.571 V gate-bump case. | Four trials with added PCB inductance were inconclusive even with added damping and a smaller time step. Qualify switch stress and local rail current with layout-derived impedance and a converged commutation model. Verify decoder and isolator timing, temperature, load, clamp and short-circuit coverage before release. [Full-bridge startup and channel overlap](circuits/battery-port/BENCH-BOOTSTRAP.md) |
| Bench PCB | The rear-bypass PCB passes ERC, DRC, via clearance and schematic parity. The two fast bypasses estimate to 1.20 nH in parallel including assumed capacitor inductance. Nominal simulated short peaks stayed below 54 V across tested Gear step sizes. Xyce and ngspice agree on about 177 ns of gate separation in the standalone control at the firmware's 200 ns command gap. Xyce's routed replay timed out. The high-side bias remains sensitive to solver and input handling, and the reduced-model hot Q5-short case exceeds 54 V. | Correlate routed bias, verify switch safe operating area, and close hot short and inductive switching. Connect the external bias supplies, receiver and rail monitors in the parent bench design, then verify independent DC interruption, thermal limits and mechanical fit before fabrication. [Independent solver results](circuits/battery-port/BENCH-SECONDARY-PROTECTED.md#independent-xyce-verification) |
| STM32 bench control | Both flash binaries execute on a Cortex-M4 instruction emulator with scripted register behavior. Tests cover arming, both ramp directions, software response to lost permit and to the latched fault flag, and a missing preload event. | Exercise the final target timing, watchdog and resets against a validated peripheral model or subsequent board capture. Extend the state coverage for closed-loop current regulation, brownout and cold start. |
| BMS, production pack and full-power ports | Preliminary 16S cabinet and DC-link design studies, plus bench-level current sharing models. | Model cell dispersion, temperature, BMS limits and disconnects. Simulate one full-power port with the selected magnetics, switches, precharge, disconnect and clamps. Model four active ports, loss of one port, stale communications and changes in allowable charge or discharge power. |
| PV MPPT and import-only grid charger | System targets and published reference designs. | Simulate full panel voltage and temperature envelope, shading and curtailment. Simulate rectifier and input filter, grid variation, phase loss, line transients, current limits and reverse-energy blocking. |
| Three-phase inverter, neutral and bypass | Architecture and reference circuits. | Model modulation, output filter and control stability with uneven phase loads, motor starts, harmonics, overloads, faults and neutral interruption. Model source transfer timing and a stuck or inconsistent contactor. |
| Complete base and PCB release | Bench schematic ERC and initial footprint assignments. | Run integrated source, battery, link, transfer and load cases. Review component stress, stored energy, insulation coordination, enclosure and thermal design. Review routed boards, run DRC, and inspect exported Gerber, drill and assembly files against the approved BOM and footprint mapping. |

CI is configured to run the base circuit regression on ngspice 42 and the two compiled firmware images against the Unicorn register fixture. The KiCad 10 job checks ERC, DRC and schematic parity on the parent and rear-bypass projects, then regenerates and refills the return-plane trial and runs a DC extraction smoke test. The locally published vendor replays use ngspice 47 with TI models in PSpice compatibility mode or Xyce with the documented adaptations. The firmware fixture uses scripted peripheral behavior. Actual HRTIM timing, independent hardware shutdown and physical fault response need bench correlation. Record the solver version, model source and result for each claim. [CI workflow](.github/workflows/verify.yml) · [Circuit checks](circuits/battery-port/README.md) · [Firmware tests](circuits/battery-port/BENCH-HRTIM.md)

The software release evidence supports fabrication review. It cannot establish physical shock safety, insulation integrity or workmanship. After fabrication, a qualified power-electronics lab should own the high-energy power-up and safety tests. [Safe isolation and stored electrical energy](https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/priced/hsg85.pdf)

## Repository audit follow-up

| Finding | Verified change or remaining work |
| --- | --- |
| Return paths, copper width and clearance | The parent project has explicit rules. A reproducible trial adds filled return planes, wider inner winding paths, four mounting holes and three fiducials. It passes native ERC, DRC, schematic parity and the 256-via land check. The filled DC extraction covers both return planes and the selected rail and winding paths. Assembly fit, steady-state temperatures and routed switching remain open. [PCB audit and trial](circuits/battery-port/BENCH-PCB.md#return-plane-trial-and-design-rules) · [Extraction results](circuits/battery-port/BENCH-PCB.md#electrical-extraction-of-the-filled-trial) |
| Power switches and BOM | Both the parent 60 V TO-220 device and the pin-compatible 100 V candidate pass the startup screen at 60, 100 and 150 nF effective bootstrap capacitance in both directions. The isolated leg uses a separate 100 V SON device. Exact part numbers and voltage ratings are specified for the previously unrated capacitors. Confirm the chosen capacitor's effective capacitance and qualify the 100 V candidate's routed switch stress, mounting and thermal path before changing the parent BOM. [Parent parts and startup screen](circuits/battery-port/BENCH-PARTS.md) · [Isolated switch study](circuits/battery-port/BENCH-SECONDARY-PROTECTED.md) |
| Firmware and CI | Both images build and pass the register emulator on Zig 0.17.1 and 0.13.0, including safe outputs and fault indication on a processor exception. The workflow pins Zig and CMSIS and checks the firmware, KiCad projects, circuit regression and local link targets. Physical HRTIM and watchdog timing need external capture. [Firmware procedure](circuits/battery-port/BENCH-HRTIM.md) · [Workflow runs](https://github.com/ramamoo3/Helios/actions/workflows/verify.yml) |
